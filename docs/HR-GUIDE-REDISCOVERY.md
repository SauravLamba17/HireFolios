# Finding past candidates again — guide for HR

This guide explains the **Rediscovery** part of the app: how to read a candidate's story, use suggestions and tags,
call people back politely, and build a call list when bulk work arrives. Nothing here is required during a drive:
intake and interviews work exactly as before.

## 1. Find a person in seconds

Type in the box at the top of every page (or press **/** to jump to it):

- 2 or more letters of the name (small typing mistakes are fine), or 4 or more digits of the mobile.
- Use the arrow keys and **Enter** to open a profile, **Esc** to close.
- Each row shows the mobile and city (to tell two people with the same name apart), the last position, the last
  visit, the hiring summary, score change, tags and "Due for call-back".

## 2. Read the Summary card (top of every profile)

- **Who they are**: age, city, education, experience, certifications, software, languages, other offer in hand.
- **The one-line summary**, for example *Not hired — Cost / salary expectation: asked 4.2 vs budget 3.0 LPA (+40%) ·
  reached Manager round · avg 4.1/5*.
- **Visits at a glance**: one line per visit (date, position, rounds, result, reason, average score, best and weakest
  area).
- **Score change** between the last two visits (↑ ↓ →); "different rounds" means the visits had different rounds.
- **Data**: when the data is deleted, or why it is kept (employee, talent-pool consent until a date).
- Tags, call-back, last contact, joining-risk hints and talent-pool consent (HR, managers and admins only).

## 3. Record why we did not hire

When an interviewer or manager rejects, the **reject reason** is still the only thing required. The reason's
**category** appears under it. For a cost reason, two optional boxes appear: the candidate's expected CTC (filled in
from the profile) and our budget. Fill them in: the suggestions, cost report and bulk lists use them.

For a **declined offer**, choose why (optional) on the profile's offer box.

## 4. Suggestions and tags

- **Suggestions to review** (Rediscovery → Suggestions, and a purple box on the profile) proposes a tag with the reason:
  - *Good but high cost*: rejected for cost with an average score of 3.5 or more.
  - *Near-miss*: rejected for a reason that is not about skills (cost, timing, shift, seat filled) after reaching the
    last round or scoring 3.5+, or the person declined our offer.
- **Accept** adds the tag; **Dismiss** hides it until there is a new decision for that person.
- You can also add a tag by hand on any decided candidate, and remove one (a note is required).
- **Do not rehire** needs a reason. When that person walks in again, a red box warns HR at the mobile number step. The
  candidate never sees it; you may still register them after checking with the manager.

## 5. Contacting people politely

On a profile (Contact and call-backs), in the pool or on a push:

- **Call** opens the phone app; **WhatsApp** opens WhatsApp with a ready message (English or Hindi) — only if the person
  agreed to WhatsApp updates, otherwise "Call only". **Mark contacted** records a contact made another way.
- After each contact, save the purpose and outcome. Everything is kept in the contact log.
- If someone was contacted recently (30 days by default), the app asks "Contacted 12 days ago by … — contact again?".
  You can continue; it never blocks.
- If the person asks not to be contacted: **They asked not to be contacted…** → write what they said. They disappear
  from the pool, bulk lists and call-backs. Only an admin can undo it, with a note.

## 6. Call-backs

- Set a call-back on the profile (suggested after a notice-period, timing or shift reason or a hold; the date is the
  decision day plus their notice period).
- **Rediscovery → Call-backs** lists what is due today, oldest first. Each row has Call / WhatsApp, **Done** (with the
  outcome) and **Snooze** (a new date). The Candidates page shows a yellow box when call-backs are due.

## 7. Consent after the visit

- If a person agrees later (in person, WhatsApp, phone or email) to be kept for future openings: profile →
  **Record consent given now**. They join the talent pool and are kept for 24 months from the later of their last visit
  and this consent. **Renew consent** starts the 24 months again.
- If they withdraw: **Withdraw consent**. They leave the pool, lists and call-backs, and the normal deletion dates start
  from that day. Settings → Rediscovery shows who will expire in the next 30 days.

## 8. Bulk hiring: build a call list

1. Rediscovery → **Bulk hiring**: choose positions (optional), number needed, maximum budget (LPA), and optionally
   certifications, software, minimum experience, night shift / voice process.
2. **Build the list**. Every row says why the person is on it, for example *Cost reject · asked 3.8 ≤ budget 4.0 · avg
   4.2 · CPC · Epic · night OK*. People up to 10% over budget appear lower with "+x% over budget".
3. "Always left out" shows how many were not listed: do not rehire, do not contact, consent withdrawn, already
   employees, in an active visit, past the data period without consent, too far over budget.
4. **Save push** with a name (e.g. "AR callers — Dec 2026"). On the push page, call people and set each status: To call,
   Called — no answer, Interested, Not interested, Booked for interview, Hired. The progress line updates.
5. **Export to Excel** is available on the pool, the bulk list and the push (no photos; every export is logged).

## 9. Joining soon and risk hints

Rediscovery → **Joining soon** lists selected people who have not joined, earliest joining date first, with hints such
as "Other offer in hand", "Notice period 90 days", "Expects 4.0 LPA, offered 3.4 LPA (+18%)" or "Joining date 45 days
away". Hints are reasons to call and check; they never change a result. The same hints show on the profile and on the
drive board's Selected list.

## 10. Reports

Reports now also show: why we didn't hire (by category and position), the cost gap, pool size by tag, suggestions
accepted vs dismissed, call-backs, push results (called → interested → booked → hired) and whether the joining-risk
hints matched what happened.

## Settings to confirm (admin, Settings → Rediscovery)

All values start as **Sample** until an admin saves them: strong score 3.5 · cooling period 30 days · talent-pool keep
period 24 months · default call-back 30 days · risk hints (other offer, notice ≥ 60 days, CTC gap ≥ 15 %, joining > 30
days, city check off) · office city · look back 24 months · 10 % over budget allowed · ranking weights (budget 30, score
25, skills 20, shift 10, improvement 5, recency 10) · six WhatsApp messages in English and Hindi. Reject categories and
tags are in Settings → Lists.
